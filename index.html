Sacred Traditions

Explore the ancient rituals and modern celebrations of Mahashivratri

Jagaran Night

Stay awake all night in devotion, chanting "Om Namah Shivaya" and meditating on Lord Shiva's divine consciousness.

Bilva Puja

Offer sacred Bilva leaves to Shiva Lingam, each leaf representing the three eyes of Shiva and purification of mind, body, and soul.

Cosmic Dance

Witness and celebrate the Tandava - Shiva's cosmic dance that sustains the rhythm of the universe and destroys ignorance.

Abhishekam

Sacred bathing of Shiva Lingam with milk, honey, yogurt, ghee, and water, symbolizing purification and divine blessings.

Bhajan Sandhya

Evening of devotional songs and chants dedicated to Lord Shiva, creating a spiritual atmosphere of divine love and devotion.

Meditation

Deep meditation on Shiva's formless aspect, seeking inner peace, self-realization, and connection with universal consciousness.
